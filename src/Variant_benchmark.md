## Manuscript truth-set design (not yet implemented in stage 5)

The benchmarking subset comprises seven KOR samples for which short-read
sequencing (SRS), PacBio LRS, and genome assemblies are all available:
**KOR-001, KOR-002, KOR-006, KOR-007, KOR-008, KOR-009, and KOR-010**
(in ascending sample-number order).

### Small-variant truth sets (Tables S28–S29)

PacBio LRS reads were aligned to GRCh38, and DeepVariant (v1.6)
was used to call SNPs and 1–50 bp indels. Retain only autosomal
(`chr1`–`chr22`) calls satisfying all of the following:

- `FILTER=PASS`, `GQ ≥20`, and `DP ≥7`.
- Heterozygous alternate-allele fraction (VAF) from **0.30 to 0.70**,
  inclusive.
- Homozygous alternate VAF **≥0.90**.

These variant sets and the subsequent benchmarking comparisons are
specified in the manuscript but are not generated by the current
stage 5 placeholder.

### Structural-variant truth sets (Table S30)

For SVs, use the same four callers and initial quality criteria as
[stage 1](genome_assembly_and_statistics.md#35-structural-variant-discovery-and-integration):
pbsv (`DP ≥7`, ALT-supporting reads `≥3`), Sniffles (`GQ ≥20`,
`DR+DV ≥7`, `DV ≥3`), SVIM (`QUAL ≥10`, `DP ≥7`,
ALT-supporting reads `≥3`), and SVIM-asm. Restrict all four
callsets to `FILTER=PASS` autosomal SVs of **≥51 bp**.

Compare each read-based caller against SVIM-asm with
`truvari bench` (reference distance **≤500 bp**, size similarity
**≥70%**, sequence similarity **≥70%**). For each sample, generate
three separate SVIM-asm-baseline truth sets requiring:

- **≥1-caller:** one or more of pbsv, Sniffles, and SVIM matches.
- **≥2-caller:** two or more matches (**primary SV truth set**).
- **3-caller:** all three read-based callers match.

Include only **INS and DEL** variants and require that **at least
one matching read-based caller** additionally satisfies
ALT-supporting reads **≥5** and `DP ≥10`. This stricter
support requirement is **additional** to the initial caller-level
criteria and is specific to benchmarking truth sets; it is not
applied to the stage 1 cohort-shared SV analysis.

The reusable stage 1 helper
`src/select_svim_asm_truvari.py` accepts `--min-read-callers`
values 1, 2, or 3 and can require `--strong-matched-vcf` files
for the extra read-support criterion. These are helper capabilities,
**not** a claim that all seven truth sets, the small-variant sets,
or the manuscript's benchmarking statistics have been generated.

## Software

The design uses pbmm2 v1.17, DeepVariant v1.6, pbsv v2.9.0,
Sniffles v2.2, SVIM v2.0.0, SVIM-asm v1.0.3 and Truvari bench.
The exact Truvari version should be recorded from the analysis environment.
