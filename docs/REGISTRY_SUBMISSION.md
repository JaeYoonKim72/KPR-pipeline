KPR-pipeline contains eight workflows for genome assembly and quality assessment, ensemble gene annotation, pangenome construction, locus- and allele-level variant comparison, variant benchmarking and Mendelian genotype inheritance inconsistency, short-read mapping, AMY structural haplotype analysis, and AMY flanking-region population genetics.

Keywords: Korean pangenome, pangenomics, genome assembly, long-read sequencing, structural variation, graph genome, variant benchmarking, GIAB, AMY locus, population genetics.
